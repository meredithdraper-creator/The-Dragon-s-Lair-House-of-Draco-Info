How the House fits together

Weekly Den Keepers is the homeroom on duty each week to tidy the hallway and make sure every locker is fully closed. Vault Rotation is a separate weekly rotation where one homeroom handles the full locker cleanout, supported by our Den Guides — students who've signed up to help clean out and organize lockers year-round. Wisdom Keepers are students who've volunteered to help classmates study for classes, organize classwork, or review notes. Loyalty Flame Keepers are students — assigned by their HR or volunteering — who share gratitude with this month's spotlighted department.
